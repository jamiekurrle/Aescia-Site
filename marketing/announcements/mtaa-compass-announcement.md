## Long version (~250 words) — company blog and LinkedIn article

> Aescia has enrolled in the Medical Technology Association of Australia's MedTech Compass programme. Compass is a structured pathway for Australian medtech companies working through concept-to-adoption: regulatory, reimbursement, clinical evidence, and post-market evidence generation. The framing matches the four surfaces we already organise around for Aescia for Hospitals.
>
> Aescia for Hospitals is an investigational software-as-a-medical-device for structured post-discharge monitoring, intended Class IIa under TGA Rule 3.4. The product is in clinical evaluation through the SAFE-Discharge trial at the Royal Prince Alfred Hospital cardiothoracic surgical unit in Sydney, registered ACTRN12625001425482. A regulatory submission has not yet been lodged. Compass enrolment does not change that posture; it formalises the regulatory and adoption work that runs alongside the trial.
>
> Aescia for Clinics is a workflow tool for specialty clinics, and is not a medical device. The MTAA programme is relevant primarily to the regulated product line.
>
> Compass enrolment sits inside a clear trajectory. We joined District 3 at Concordia University in September 2025 — that is the Montréal anchor for our R&D, talent, and Canadian data-residency work. The MTAA programme is the Sydney-side counterpart, anchored in the country that is home to our regulatory applicant entity, Aescia Pty Ltd, and to the trial. The two programmes are complementary by design: District 3 around the engineering and clinical-pathway-authoring cadence, MedTech Compass around the regulatory and reimbursement cadence.
>
> We will publish progress on the Updates page as it lands. Compass is a programme, not an endorsement. Joining it earns us nothing clinically — that is what the trial is for.

(~270 words.)
