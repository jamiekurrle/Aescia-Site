Prize draw terms

Colonoscopy Experience Survey — Aescia

  1. Promoter. Aescia Pty Ltd (ABN 96 687 840 517), 15 Amber Place, Bass Hill NSW 2197, Australia. Contact: james@aescia.com.
  2. Prize. One (1) digital gift card to the value of A$100, delivered by email. There is one prize in total. No cash alternative, and the prize is not transferable.
  3. Who can enter. People who complete the survey, tick the interview opt-in, and leave a valid email address (residents of Australia, the United States, or Canada may leave a phone number instead). Entrants must be 18 or over and resident in Australia, the United States, Canada, New Zealand, the United Kingdom, or Ireland. Employees of Aescia and their immediate families are not eligible.
  4. How to enter. Submit the survey with the interview opt-in ticked and leave a valid email address (or a phone number if you're in Australia, the United States, or Canada). One entry per person: if more than one entry shares the same email address or phone number, only the earliest is counted. No purchase or payment is required or possible.
  5. The draw. The survey closes on 14 August 2026. The winner is drawn at random from all eligible entries within 14 days of the close, by Aescia.
  6. Canada. To claim, a Canadian winner must correctly answer a time-limited mathematical skill-testing question.
  7. Notification and claim. The winner is contacted using the details they provided within 7 days of the draw. If the winner cannot be reached or does not respond within 14 days, they forfeit the prize and a replacement winner is drawn from the remaining eligible entries.
  8. Odds of winning depend on the number of eligible entries received.
  9. Privacy. Entry details are handled per the survey privacy statement and used only to run this draw and, if you opted in, to contact you about the research.
  10. General. Aescia may end or amend this draw if required for reasons beyond its reasonable control. This draw is not sponsored or endorsed by any gift-card provider.
