Your colonoscopy experience

A short survey, a few minutes. There are no wrong answers; we're learning what the experience is really like. At the end you can leave your details if you're open to telling us more.

Before you start

This short survey is part of research by Aescia into what the colonoscopy experience is really like, led by Dr James Kurrle. Taking part is voluntary: you can skip any question and stop at any time. Your answers and any contact details are stored securely and used only for this research; you can ask us to delete them any time at james@aescia.com.

This survey is research, not medical care. If you have a health concern, please contact your doctor or your local emergency service.

How we handle your information

Who: this survey is run by Aescia Pty Ltd (ABN 96 687 840 517), 15 Amber Place, Bass Hill NSW 2197, led by Dr James Kurrle, for research into the colonoscopy experience.

What we collect: your survey answers about your colonoscopy experience (health information), including your country and region, plus which shared link you arrived from. Your name, email, and phone are collected only if you tick an option below and choose to give them.

Why: to understand what the experience is really like and how to improve care around it. If you tick an option, to send you research updates and/or invite you to a short interview and prize draw. We use your details only for the option you tick.

Where it's stored: in Google Workspace (Google Drive), which may store or process data on servers outside your country, including the United States. Access is limited to the Aescia research team.

Sharing: we don't sell your information or use it for advertising. Anything we publish or present is combined and de-identified so you can't be recognised.

How long: we keep survey answers for up to 24 months, then delete or de-identify them. We delete contact details within 3 months after the interviews and prize draw are finished.

Your choices: taking part is voluntary: you can skip questions and stop at any time. You can ask to see, correct, or delete your information by emailing james@aescia.com. If you don't leave contact details, your response isn't linked to your name (though details like your region or free-text answers could still indirectly identify you), and we won't be able to find it later to delete it.

Questions or complaints: email james@aescia.com. You can also contact your local privacy regulator.

Not medical care: this survey doesn't provide medical advice. If you have a health concern, please contact your doctor or your local emergency service.

If you've had more than one colonoscopy, answer across all of them: tell us about anything that stands out, even if different things happened at different times.
