Two of these inputs have published support and two do not. The cancellation range and the 8.6 percent thirty-day encounter rate come from the literature. The share of slots we refill and the share of encounters we prevent are assumptions with no published basis for anyone in this category, ours included. Pricing

We charge less than we improve your economic situation.

  • Early sites are free. We need the evidence more than the revenue.
  • After that, a per-case rate set once we have measured the recovery on your own log.
  • Whatever an early site is paying is the lowest price we offer anyone.
  • Cancellations. Both published figures are gross. The American Society for Gastrointestinal Endoscopy's 2019 benchmarking survey counts no-shows plus cancellations inside 48 hours at 5.6 to 8.45 percent of all scheduled; HST Pathways 2024 reports 22 percent across 207 centers, counting any reschedule. No published source measures the slots that stayed empty.
  • Margin, 62 percent. HST Pathways 2024: $1,420 net revenue a case against roughly $888 facility margin.
  • Denials, 10 percent. HST Pathways 2024, up three points year on year.
  • Inadequate prep, 5 to 12 percent. GI Quality Improvement Consortium registry: 5.3 percent of 3,773,519 colonoscopies, flat at 5.2 to 5.5 percent since 2015, and 79.9 percent of them done in a surgery or endoscopy center. The 2024 gastroenterology society quality indicator and the 2025 US Multi-Society Task Force consensus both set the target at 90 percent adequate.
  • Total joint facility fee. Journal of Arthroplasty 2025: about $10,204 knee and $9,983 hip through 2024, less roughly 7.8 percent in 2026.
  • Total joint same-day cancellation, about 1 percent. 362 of 38,849 scheduled cases, 2017 to 2023.
  • THA/TKA PRO-PM. Mandatory under ASCQR from 2027. Pre-operative within 90 days before, post-operative 300 to 425 days after, 50 percent minimum reporting rate.
  • ASCQR penalty. Two percentage point reduction to the annual ASC Fee Schedule payment rate update.
  • 96-hour annual average. Medicare condition of participation. Swing-bed and distinct-part-unit days excluded.
  • Method II at 115 percent. Social Security Act section 1834(g)(2)(B).
