PODSS Comparison - Manual vs Aescia-Augmented Post-Discharge Monitoring

PODSS (Post-Operative Discharge Support Service) is a Queensland Health nurse-led telehealth program created after NSQIP data showed 10% of surgical patients present to ED within 30 days. It uses Clinical Nurse Consultants (CNCs) for proactive and reactive phone follow-up. Currently active at 12+ Queensland hospitals including GCUH (self-funded). Source: Clinical Excellence QLD

Pre-Specialist Appointment Monitoring

When a GP refers a patient to a specialist, wait times can be months. Aescia monitors patients during the wait - detecting deterioration that needs urgent escalation, identifying if imaging/bloods should be done beforehand, and potentially enabling earlier triage. The specialist appointment becomes more productive when the patient arrives with a monitoring history.

Clinical Trial & Research Monitoring

Clinical trials require rigorous participant monitoring, adverse event capture, and protocol adherence tracking. Aescia automates daily check-ins, captures PROs (Patient Reported Outcomes), flags adverse events in real-time, and reduces the cost per enrolled patient while improving data completeness.
